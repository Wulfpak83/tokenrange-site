~/tokenrange ❯ faq

questions, answered straight.

How do I check my Claude Code rate limit on iPhone?

Install Token Range and sign in to Claude once. Your session and weekly limits appear live in the app, on Home Screen widgets, and on the Lock Screen, with reset countdowns.

Does Token Range see my credentials or usage data?

No. Credentials live in your device Keychain and are sent only to the provider you connected, over HTTPS. There is no Token Range server, no analytics, and no account to create.
