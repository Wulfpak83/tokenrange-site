Support

Token Range for iOS 17 and later, and macOS 13 and later

support@tokenrange.app

One developer reads this. Expect a reply within two business days. Include your iOS or macOS version, your Token Range version (Settings, at the bottom), and which provider is misbehaving.

common questions

A provider will not connect.
  1. Confirm you can sign in to that provider in a browser on the same device. Token Range uses the provider's own sign-in page, so if that page fails, the connection cannot succeed.
  2. In Settings, disconnect the provider and connect it again. Sign-in tokens expire and a stale one cannot always refresh itself.
  3. If your account uses SSO through an employer, the provider may block third-party sign-in entirely. That restriction sits with the provider.
My iPhone widget shows an older percentage than the app.

This is expected, and the widget says so. iOS decides when a widget may refresh, and it will not wake an app on demand. The countdown to your next reset keeps ticking on its own, but the percentage is only as fresh as the last time the app refreshed. Open Token Range, or tap refresh, and the widget catches up.

I paid for Lifetime Pro but the app still looks locked.

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to the Apple ID that bought them, so check you are signed into that same Apple ID. If it still does not unlock, email your App Store receipt and it will be sorted out.

Is Lifetime Pro a subscription?

No. It is one payment and it does not renew. Apple shows the local price before purchase. There is no monthly or yearly plan and there is no plan to add one.

What happens to my credentials?

They are written to the Apple Keychain on your iPhone or Mac and are sent only to the provider that issued them. There is no Token Range server, no account, and no analytics. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

Why are the numbers percentages instead of tokens or dollars?

Providers report how much of a limit window remains and when it resets. Some also report spend or balance details; Token Range shows those only when the provider returns them. Providers do not report token counts, so Token Range does not invent them.

My history is empty.

Tracking starts the day you connect. History is never backfilled or invented, so a new install genuinely has nothing to show yet.

How do I delete my data?

First, disconnect every provider inside Token Range. That removes each Keychain credential and current connection record. Then uninstall the app. Apple may retain Keychain items or local Mac container data after an app is removed, which is why disconnecting first matters. If Token Range is already uninstalled, email support for platform-specific cleanup steps. The developer holds no copy and cannot erase this local data remotely.

Which tools does Token Range read?

Five at launch: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Grok, and Antigravity. One connected account is free forever; Lifetime Pro unlocks additional accounts.

bugs and requests

Both go to the same address. A bug report gets fixed faster with the steps you took, what you expected, and what happened instead. Feature requests are read and genuinely do shape what gets built.

Provider names identify the services you choose to connect and remain the trademarks of their owners. If a provider changes its sign-in or usage service, that provider's meter may pause until Token Range is updated. Other provider connections and your local data remain separate.

refunds

Purchases run through Apple, so refunds do too. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and pick the purchase. The developer cannot issue an App Store refund directly. If something is broken, email first and it may not need one.
